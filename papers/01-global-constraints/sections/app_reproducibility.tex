\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:reproducibility}

All computations used in the physics translation are exact integer, rational
or symbolic calculations. No numerical fit or floating-point rank decision
enters a claim. From the project root, the local checks are run with
\begin{verbatim}
venv/bin/python checks/check_nodal_charge.py
venv/bin/python checks/check_e2_gauging.py
venv/bin/python checks/check_e3_gauging.py
venv/bin/python checks/check_e3_magnetic_quivers.py
venv/bin/python checks/check_xcirc_e3_profiles.py
venv/bin/python checks/check_x9_spectrum.py
python3 checks/check_smoothing_criterion.py
\end{verbatim}
The scripts verify, respectively:
\begin{enumerate}
 \item the nodal moment-map image and charge-kernel identity;
 \item the $E_2$ invariant ring, balanced lift and $X_9$ reduced matrix;
 \item the $E_3$ invariant ring, root basis and branchwise balanced lifts;
 \item the affine-$A_1/A_2$ monopole sums and fiber-product Hilbert-series
 identity;
 \item the four $X^{\circ}$ profile ranks, Smith invariants and isolating
 vectors;
 \item the $X_9$ divisor quotient, Hodge numbers, effective vectors and
 multiplet changes; and
 \item the three $E_3$ discriminant factors in the moment-map root
 convention, the finite row-space smoothing test, and the four
 profile restrictions of \eqref{eq:discriminant-example-gaugings}.
\end{enumerate}

The source geometric computations are maintained with the companion
mathematical papers. For the thirty-sector obstruction they include the
independent reconstructions of the $36\times26$ matrix, its topological kernel,
the local Milnor kernels and the vanishing-period statements. For $X_9$ they
include the singular-locus calculation, branch restriction, explicit family,
divisor quotient and Batyrev lattice-point counts
\cite{Wuebben:2026obstruction,Wuebben:2026smoothing}.

The finite scripts check the printed matrices and
formulas. They do not replace the Hodge-theoretic proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:first-order-image}, the analytic necessity and sufficiency
in Theorem~\ref{thm:all-orders-necessity}, selected-base integrability, or
the geometric construction of the $X_9$ family. The full toric input and
deformation-base calculation for the thirty-node/two-cone counterexample,
as well as the $X_{19}$ smoothing, are reproduced in the companion
mathematical source distribution \cite{Wuebben:2026smoothing}.

The manuscript source and exact finite checks are available at
\url{https://github.com/bwuebben/global-higgs-branch}.
