\title{The Higgs branch of a compact Calabi--Yau\\transition at finite Planck mass}
\author{Bernd Johannes Wuebben}
\affiliation{New York, NY, USA}
\emailAdd{wuebben@gmail.com}
\date{}

\abstract{When a compact Calabi--Yau threefold degenerates, M-theory on it acquires light charged
hypermultiplets whose condensation opens a Higgs branch, locally a hyperk\"ahler quotient without gravity. At finite Planck mass the hypermultiplet moduli space is quaternionic K\"ahler, its
metric is unknown, and its moment maps have no additive constant, hence no Fayet--Iliopoulos
parameter. We prove a local normal form for quaternionic K\"ahler quotients near a
fixed point at which the moment map vanishes, under a freeness condition on the normal
representation: the quotient is locally the
product of the fixed set with the flat hyperk\"ahler quotient of that representation, with relative
metric corrections of order $r^2$ at distance $r$. Consequently, if exactly $k$ disjoint
$(-1,-1)$-curves have zero area on a face of the nef cone with finite gauge couplings, and their
classes satisfy one primitive relation $\sum_ia_i[C_i]=0$ with all $a_i\neq0$, then, under a regularity
hypothesis on the effective supergravity, the Higgs branch opened by the $k$ light hypermultiplets is locally a smooth factor times $\CC^2/\ZZ_m$, $m=\sum_i|a_i|$, as without gravity.
Transverse to the smooth factor, the order-$r^2$ correction is the curvature of the hypermultiplet manifold restricted to a
quaternionic line. A transition with two nodes and a cone over the degree-seven del Pezzo surface has
such a face with $k=4$, giving $\CC^2/\ZZ_4$; where the del Pezzo surface collapses, the Higgs branch defined as the metric
completion of the vacua on the smoothed threefold is again of this form. In type IIA string
theory, under corresponding hypotheses, the $\CC^2/\ZZ_k$ point that Greene, Morrison and Vafa expected
near $k$ homologous vanishing three-cycles persists at finite Planck mass for these smoothings. A
census of toric hypersurfaces finds 5605 faces whose light spectrum has one relation, 372 with
$k\geq3$, all with $m=k$.}
