\title{Bulk Gauging and Quantum Couplings\\
across a Compact Higgs Transition}
\author{Bernd Johannes Wuebben}
\affiliation{New York, NY, USA}
\emailAdd{wuebben@gmail.com}
\abstract{We study an explicit compact Calabi--Yau transition in which two
conifold sectors and a rank-one $E_2$ sector can Higgs only together.
The integral relation between their curve classes determines two shared
Abelian gaugings and a transition from Hodge numbers $(6,122)$ to
$(3,123)$. We compute the primitive surviving charge lattice and its
cubic couplings, and show that the flavor gauging cannot remain
dynamical in a gravity-decoupling limit with bounded local K\"ahler
scales. On an auxiliary circle the quantum transition contains four
conifold states spanning a primitive rank-three electric lattice.
An explicit Laurent mutation and analytic continuation of all eight
regular periods identify the genus-zero prepotential of the surviving
vectors with that of the smooth phase, including the integral
normalization and constant term. The four-state description is not
uniform in the five-dimensional limit because the Kaluza--Klein gap
closes. The K3 and elliptic fibrations identify a dilaton gauging,
an anomaly-consistent six-dimensional $U(2)$ phase and equal compact
images of two E-string roots. An exact volume bound excludes the
six-dimensional limit on the transition face. A heterotic K3 candidate
and topological data are obtained; the global bundle construction
and the finite-Planck hypermultiplet geometry remain open.}
\keywords{M-Theory, Supersymmetric Gauge Theory, Supergravity Models,
String Duality, Differential and Algebraic Geometry}
