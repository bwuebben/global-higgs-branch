\section{Quantum vector multiplets across the transition}
\label{sec:quantum}

Compactify the five-dimensional theory on an auxiliary spacetime
circle. Mirror symmetry then gives access to its four-dimensional
vector multiplets through periods of a compact Calabi--Yau threefold.
We construct a four-node degeneration, fix its integral charges in
the large-volume frame, and identify the surviving eight periods
with those of the smooth side of Section~\ref{subsec:x9}.
All analytic identities below refer to a specified continuation
branch with small nonzero spectator parameters. The final subsection
takes the auxiliary radius to infinity at fixed M-theory volume.
